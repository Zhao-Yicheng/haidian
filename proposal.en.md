# Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt - Conceptual Urban Design Proposal

## Abstract

This proposal responds to the worlds first Agent-native urban design open call for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt. Haidian District offers 43.6 km2 of real urban territory from the North Fifth Ring Road to Beijing North Railway Station, linking Qinghuayuan, Dazhongsi, and the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park. The proposal establishes an overall structure of One Smart-Green Spine, Three Core Districts, and Six Supporting Wings, envisioning a new urban form, innovation ecosystem, and deployable scenarios for the AI era.

## 1. Design Basis and Source Inventory

### 1.1 Project Background

In 1909, Zhan Tianyou oversaw the construction of the Jing-Zhang Railway, the first trunk railway line independently designed and built by Chinese engineers. A century later, the land connected by this railway heritage is becoming a new origin point for the AI era. In 2026, Haidian District launched the worlds first Agent-only urban design open call along the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park corridor.

### 1.2 Higher-Order Planning Context

- The Beijing City Master Plan (2016-2035) positions Haidian as the core zone of the National Science and Innovation Center with global influence.
- The Haidian 14th Five-Year Plan strengthens the leading role of Zhongguancun Science City in original and independent innovation.
- The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park Special Plan establishes the overall vision of a Century-Old Railway, Cultural Green Spine.

## 3. Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future-City Strategy

### 3.1 Development Vision

Drawing on science cities, tech parks, and innovation districts worldwide (Silicon Valley, Tsukuba, Shenzhen Nanshan, Shanghai Zhangjiang, Singapore one-north), we propose a development vision suited to Haidian: an AI Independent Innovation Origin, a Talent-Friendly Vibrant City, and a Digital-Physical Fusion Proving Ground.

### 3.2 Industry Ecosystem

| Industry Priority | Spatial Organization | Core Indicator |
|---|---|---|

| Embodied intelligence and robotics | Dazhongsi A3 industry cluster | Number of test scenarios |
| AI + health / education / transport | A2 AI Origin Community | Number of application scenarios |

### 3.3 Five Reference Cases

1. **Silicon Valley, USA**: Industry-research closed loop and venture capital ecology.
2. **Tsukuba Science City, Japan**: Concentrated national research facilities.
3. **Shenzhen Nanshan Science Park, China**: Market-driven rapid iteration.
4. **Shanghai Zhangjiang Science City, China**: Major science facilities and industrial synergy.
5. **Singapore one-north**: Work-live-learn-play integration.

## 4. Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Design

### 4.1 Design Positioning

Using the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park as a green spine, the design stitches the eastern and western urban districts and connects northern and southern innovation elements into an innovation vitality belt for the AI era.

### 4.2 Three-Core Structure

| District | Area | Positioning |
|---|---|---|
| A1 Zhongzhiyuan | 192.1 ha | AI independent innovation acceleration area |
| A2 Beijing AI Origin Community | 104.3 ha | Talent vitality core |
| A3 Dazhongsi | 72.0 ha | AI industry cluster |

## 5. Detailed Design for the Three Key Areas

### 5.1 A1 Zhongzhiyuan - AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area

Houses a national AI computing center, an open-source foundation-model community, and an AI safety evaluation platform. Existing industrial relics are preserved and adapted into the Pioneers Forest, serving as an AI pilgrimage landmark.

### 5.2 A2 Beijing AI Origin Community

For global AI talent: international talent apartments, AI joint laboratories, and 24-hour innovation streets. The community center hosts the AI Origin Square, the permanent main venue for the annual Global AI Developer Conference.

### 5.3 A3 Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Focused on embodied intelligence, autonomous driving, and unmanned delivery, with an open-road testing field, a robotics shared factory, and an AI product pilot base.

Preserve/renovate/demolish/new-build logic: prioritize preservation of industrial relics and community fabric; renovate existing factories into innovation space; demolish only dilapidated structures without heritage value; focus new construction on key nodes.

## 8. Transport, Municipal, and Public Services

- **Transport**: Arterial-collector-local three-tier road system; a slow-mobility main axis within the heritage park; an AI autonomous shuttle loop.
- **Municipal**: Utility tunnel + district energy station + stormwater retention ponds.
- **Public services**: International talent service center, 24-hour library, community health station, childcare facilities.

## 9. Blue-Green Public Space and Urban Character

With the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park as the green spine, neighborhood green corridors extend to both sides, forming a one-spine, two-wings, multi-node blue-green network. The design blends Jing-Zhang railway culture, Zhongguancun innovation culture, and emerging AI culture into a distinctive identity of historical depth x innovation speed.
